How to play

  1. Press Start.
  2. Move your left paddle with W / S, ↑ / ↓, or the mouse.
  3. Bounce the ball past the CPU paddle on the right to score.
  4. First to the Win at score wins.
  5. Space pauses · Enter restarts.
